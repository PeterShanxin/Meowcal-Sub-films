# Keyboard samples

`key-01.wav` … `key-32.wav` are the 32 single keypresses from
"Keyboard Soundpack 1 (typing and single keystrokes)" by unicaegames,
OpenGameArt, licensed CC0:
https://opengameart.org/content/keyboard-soundpack-1-typing-and-single-keystrokes
(`unicae_games_keyboard_soundpack_1_0.zip`, SHA-256
`935eae2fa5c3742eacdd38c4ea0e9047f3887faa0701996498492c256db1b351`).
Recorded on a Cherry KC 1000 with a Shure SM7B. Converted here from
44.1 kHz to 48 kHz mono PCM; otherwise unchanged.
